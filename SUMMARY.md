## Vision

To create the most comprehensive, research-backed, practical book on habits ever written, specifically designed for the AI era.

---

## Book Structure

### 13 Parts, 51 Chapters

- Part 1: Understanding Habits (Chapters 1-4)
- Part 2: Brain Science (Chapters 5-8)
- Part 3: Psychology (Chapters 9-12)
- Part 4: Building Good Habits (Chapters 13-16)
- Part 5: Breaking Bad Habits (Chapters 17-20)
- Part 6: Health Habits (Chapters 21-24)
- Part 7: Money Habits (Chapters 25-28)
- Part 8: Learning Habits (Chapters 29-32)
- Part 9: Relationship Habits (Chapters 33-36)
- Part 10: Leadership Habits (Chapters 37-40)
- Part 11: AI Era Habits (Chapters 41-44)
- Part 12: The Habit Operating System (Chapters 45-48)
- Part 13: 365-Day Challenge (Chapters 49-51)

### 5 Appendices

- Appendix A: Habit Diagnosis Test
- Appendix B: Worksheets & Templates
- Appendix C: Habit Encyclopedia
- Appendix D: Research References
- Appendix E: Printable Resources
