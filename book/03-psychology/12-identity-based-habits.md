# Chapter 12: Identity-Based Habits - Becoming the Person You Want to Be

> **Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to become.**

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand identity-based vs outcome-based habits
2. Learn how identity shapes behavior
3. Discover identity design principles
4. Apply to habit formation

---

## Identity-Based vs Outcome-Based

Outcome: Lose weight
Identity: Become a healthy person

Outcome: Write a book
Identity: Be a writer

---

## Three Layers of Change

1. Outcomes: What you want to achieve
2. Processes: What you want to do
3. Identity: Who you want to become

Identity-based change is most powerful and sustainable.

---

## Identity Shift Process

1. Define desired identity
2. Take small actions aligned with identity
3. Collect evidence of new identity
4. Reinforce through affirmation and environment

---

## Practical Applications

1. Use identity statements: I am the type of person who...
2. Design identity-aligned actions
3. Create identity-based environment
4. Find identity tribe
5. Review identity progress regularly
