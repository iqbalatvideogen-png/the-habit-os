# Chapter 10: Cognitive Biases and How They Sabotage Your Habits

> **Your brain takes shortcuts that can lead you astray.**

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand cognitive biases
2. Learn common biases affecting habits
3. Discover how to overcome biases
4. Apply to better decision making

---

## Common Cognitive Biases

- Confirmation Bias: Seeking information that confirms beliefs
- Status Quo Bias: Preferring things to stay the same
- Sunk Cost Fallacy: Continuing due to past investment
- Present Bias: Preferring immediate over future rewards
- Overconfidence Bias: Overestimating abilities
- Planning Fallacy: Underestimating time needed

---

## Biases Affecting Habits

- Present Bias: Procrastination
- Status Quo Bias: Resistance to change
- Confirmation Bias: Ignoring contradictory evidence
- Overconfidence: Setting unrealistic goals

---

## Overcoming Biases

1. Awareness is the first step
2. Use implementation intentions
3. Create commitment devices
4. Seek outside perspectives
5. Use data and tracking
