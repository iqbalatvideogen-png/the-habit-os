# Chapter 11: The Psychology of Willpower and Self-Control

> **Willpower is like a muscle that can be strengthened.**

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand willpower as limited resource
2. Learn the ego depletion effect
3. Discover how to strengthen willpower
4. Apply to habit formation

---

## The Willpower Muscle

- Limited resource that gets depleted
- Can be strengthened with practice
- Affected by sleep, nutrition, stress

---

## Ego Depletion

- Using willpower on one task reduces it for others
- Explains why dieting makes other habits harder
- Manage by prioritizing most important tasks

---

## Strengthening Willpower

1. Practice self-control exercises
2. Get adequate sleep
3. Eat a healthy diet
4. Manage stress
5. Use implementation intentions

---

## The Willpower Workaround

Instead of relying on willpower:
1. Design your environment
2. Create automatic habits
3. Use commitment devices
4. Remove temptations
5. Build systems
