---

## The 30-Day Reset Framework

### Phase 1: Preparation (Days 1-3)
- Identify habit to change
- Understand triggers
- Design replacement
- Prepare environment

### Phase 2: Action (Days 4-27)
- Implement replacement habit
- Track daily
- Manage setbacks
- Stay accountable

### Phase 3: Integration (Days 28-30)
- Review progress
- Adjust as needed
- Plan for long-term
- Celebrate success

---

## Why 30 Days?

- Enough time to form new neural pathways
- Long enough to see results
- Short enough to stay motivated
- Creates momentum for longer change
