# Chapter 18: The Inversion Technique

> Turn your obstacles into opportunities.

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand inversion technique
2. Learn how to flip bad habits
3. Discover practical applications
4. Apply to your habits

---

## What is Inversion?

Instead of trying to stop a bad habit, transform it into a good one.

---

## How It Works

1. Identify the bad habit
2. Understand the need it fulfills
3. Find a positive alternative that meets the same need
4. Redirect the energy into the positive habit

---

## Examples

Bad: Stress eating -> Good: Stress walking
Bad: Procrastinating with TV -> Good: Procrastinating with reading
Bad: Nail biting -> Good: Fidget toy use
Bad: Smoking -> Good: Deep breathing

---

## Why It Works

- Addresses the root need
- Uses existing habit energy
- More sustainable than suppression
- Reduces resistance
