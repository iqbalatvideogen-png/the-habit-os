# Chapter 19: Digital Detox

> Disconnect to reconnect.

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand digital addiction
2. Learn detox strategies
3. Discover how to reduce digital distractions
4. Apply to your life

---

## The Problem

- Average person checks phone 96 times per day
- Spends 7 plus hours on screens
- 200 plus apps on phone
- Constant notifications fragment focus

---

## Digital Detox Strategies

### 1. Digital Audit
- List all digital habits
- Identify time wasted
- Categorize by value

### 2. Set Boundaries
- No screens before breakfast
- No screens after dinner
- No screens in bedroom
- Designated tech-free times

### 3. Reduce Friction
- Delete unused apps
- Turn off notifications
- Use grayscale mode
- Set app limits

### 4. Replace Digital Habits
- Replace scrolling with reading
- Replace social media with calls
- Replace TV with hobbies

---

## Action Checklist

- Conduct digital audit
- Delete 5 unused apps
- Turn off non-essential notifications
- Set screen time limits
- Create tech-free zones
