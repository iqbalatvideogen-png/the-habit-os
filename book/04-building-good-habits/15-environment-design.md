# Chapter 15: Environment Design - Making Good Habits Inevitable

> **Design your environment to design your life.**

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand environment influence on habits
2. Learn environment design principles
3. Discover how to make good habits easy
4. Apply to your spaces

---

## The 4 Laws of Behavior Change

Make good habits:
- Obvious
- Attractive
- Easy

Make bad habits:
- Invisible
- Unattractive
- Difficult

---

## Environment Design Strategies

1. Make Good Habits Obvious (cue design)
2. Make Good Habits Attractive (attraction design)
3. Make Good Habits Easy (friction reduction)
4. Make Bad Habits Invisible (cue removal)
5. Make Bad Habits Unattractive (attraction reduction)
6. Make Bad Habits Difficult (friction increase)
