## What Are Implementation Intentions?

If [SITUATION], then I will [BEHAVIOR]

Examples:
- If it is 7 AM, then I will exercise for 10 minutes
- If I feel stressed, then I will take 3 deep breaths
- If I walk past the kitchen, then I will drink a glass of water

---

## Why They Work

- Creates mental links
- Reduces decision fatigue
- Increases situation awareness
- Automates behavior
- Doubles or triples success rate

---

## Types of Implementation Intentions

1. Time-Based: If it is [TIME], then I will [BEHAVIOR]
2. Location-Based: If I am at [PLACE], then I will [BEHAVIOR]
3. Emotion-Based: If I feel [EMOTION], then I will [BEHAVIOR]
4. Event-Based: If [EVENT] happens, then I will [BEHAVIOR]
5. Action-Based: After I [ACTION], then I will [BEHAVIOR]
