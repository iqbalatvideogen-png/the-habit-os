## Habit Stacking Formula

After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW HABIT]

Examples:
- After I brush my teeth, I will floss one tooth
- After I pour coffee, I will write to-do list
- After I take off work shoes, I will change into workout clothes

---

## Why It Works

- Uses existing neural pathways
- No need to remember new cue
- Built-in consistency
- Higher success rate (30-50% better)
