## The 5-Step Process

1. Cue Identification
2. Craving Creation
3. Response Design
4. Reward Engineering
5. Repetition Planning

---

## Science of Habit Formation

- Average 66 days for habit to become automatic
- Physical brain changes occur
- Neural pathways strengthen with repetition

---

## Practical Strategies

1. Start with tiny habits
2. Use implementation intentions
3. Design environment
4. Track progress

---

## Common Mistakes

- Trying to change too many habits at once
- Not making habits easy enough
- Ignoring reward component
