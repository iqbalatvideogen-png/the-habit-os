# Chapter 42: Digital Minimalism

> Less but better.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand digital minimalism
2. Learn digital decluttering
3. Discover intentional tech use
4. Apply to your digital life

---

## Digital Minimalism Principles

1. Clutter is costly
2. Less is more
3. Intentionality matters
4. Quality over quantity
5. Freedom from distraction

---

## Digital Declutter

1. Audit all digital tools
2. Keep only essential
3. Optimize remaining
4. Set usage rules
5. Regular review

---

## Intentional Tech Use

1. Define purpose
2. Set limits
3. Schedule usage
4. Remove distractions
5. Review regularly

---

## Action Checklist
- Audit digital tools
- Delete unused apps
- Optimize essential tools
- Set usage rules
- Review monthly
