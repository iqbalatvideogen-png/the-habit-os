Key AI Milestones:
- 1956: Dartmouth Conference - Birth of AI as a field
- 1997: IBM Deep Blue defeats Garry Kasparov in chess
- 2011: IBM Watson wins Jeopardy
- 2016: AlphaGo defeats Lee Sedol in Go
- 2022: Stable Diffusion and DALL-E 2 - AI-generated art goes mainstream
- 2022: ChatGPT launch - 100 million users in 2 months

### How AI Works

Machine Learning is a subset of AI where systems learn from data without explicit programming.

Key ML Approaches:
1. Supervised Learning: Learns from labeled data
2. Unsupervised Learning: Finds patterns in unlabeled data
3. Reinforcement Learning: Learns by trial and error with rewards

Large Language Models are trained on vast amounts of text data with transformer architecture and attention mechanisms.

### AI Capabilities and Limitations

What AI Can Do Well:
- Pattern recognition in large datasets
- Natural language processing and generation
- Image and speech recognition
- Prediction based on historical data
- Automation of repetitive tasks
- Personalization to individual preferences

Current Limitations:
- No true understanding (Bender and Koller, 2020)
- Hallucinations: Generates plausible but incorrect information
- Bias and fairness issues (Buolamwini and Gebru, 2018)
- Limited context window and memory
- Significant energy consumption

### AI Impact on Jobs

McKinsey Global Institute (2023):

- AI will create new roles and industries

## Common Mistakes

1. Over-Reliance on AI: Blindly trusting AI outputs
2. Ignoring AI Limitations: Not fact-checking AI outputs
3. Not Developing AI Literacy: Failing to learn about AI
4. Ethical Blind Spots: Using AI without considering implications
5. Resistance to AI Adoption: Avoiding AI tools entirely
6. Poor Prompt Engineering: Writing ineffective prompts
7. Not Staying Updated: Assuming AI knowledge is complete

## Myths vs Evidence

Myth: AI will take all our jobs
Reality: AI will transform jobs, not eliminate them. 60 percent of jobs will be transformed, with new roles created.

Myth: AI is as smart as humans
Reality: AI has narrow intelligence, not general intelligence. It lacks true understanding and consciousness.

Myth: AI is always objective
Reality: AI reflects biases in its training data and can perpetuate stereotypes.

Myth: AI can solve any problem
Reality: AI excels in specific domains but has limitations in general problem-solving.

Myth: AI is only for tech companies
Reality: AI is transforming all industries from healthcare to agriculture.

Myth: AI is too expensive
Reality: Many AI tools offer free tiers and affordable options.

Myth: AI is a passing fad
Reality: AI is a fundamental technological shift with exponential growth.
