## Summary
The science and technology of habit formation are evolving rapidly. This chapter explores emerging trends and prepares you for the future of behavior change.

## Research Insights
Neuroscience is revealing new insights into habit formation. The basal ganglia plays a key role in automatic behaviors (Graybiel, 2008). Neuroplasticity allows habits to be rewired throughout life (Doidge, 2007).

Technology is enabling new approaches to habit change. Wearable devices provide real-time feedback (Patel et al., 2019). Virtual reality offers immersive habit practice environments (Slater and Sanchez-Vives, 2016).

## Myths vs Evidence
Myth: Habits cannot be changed in adulthood
Reality: Neuroplasticity allows habit change at any age (Doidge, 2007)

Myth: Willpower is the only way to change habits
Reality: Environment design and systems are more effective (Clear, 2018)
