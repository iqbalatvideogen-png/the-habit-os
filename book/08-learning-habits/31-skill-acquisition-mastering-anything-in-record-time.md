# Chapter 31: Skill Acquisition

> Skill is the unified force of experience, intellect, and passion.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand skill acquisition
2. Learn deliberate practice
3. Discover acceleration techniques
4. Apply to your skills

---

## The 10,000 Hour Rule

- Popularized by Malcolm Gladwell
- Deliberate practice required
- Not just time, but quality practice

---

## Deliberate Practice

1. Focus on weaknesses
2. Get feedback
3. Practice with intention
4. Push beyond comfort zone
5. Measure progress

---

## Acceleration Techniques

1. Break down skills
2. Learn fundamentals first
3. Use spaced repetition
4. Get coaching
5. Practice consistently

---

## Action Checklist
- Choose skill to master
- Break into sub-skills
- Create practice plan
- Get feedback
- Practice daily
