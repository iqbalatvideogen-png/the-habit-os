# Chapter 33: Communication Habits

> The art of communication is the language of leadership.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand effective communication
2. Learn communication habits
3. Discover active listening
4. Apply to relationships

---

## Effective Communication

1. Be clear and concise
2. Use I statements
3. Practice active listening
4. Show empathy
5. Be authentic

---

## Communication Habits

1. Daily check-ins
2. Weekly deep conversations
3. Monthly relationship reviews
4. Conflict resolution talks
5. Appreciation expressions

---

## Active Listening

1. Give full attention
2. Show you are listening
3. Provide feedback
4. Defer judgment
5. Respond appropriately

---

## Action Checklist
- Practice active listening daily
- Have meaningful conversations
- Express appreciation regularly
- Resolve conflicts promptly
- Review relationships monthly
