# Chapter 35: Conflict Resolution

> Peace is not the absence of conflict, but the ability to cope with it.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand conflict causes
2. Learn resolution strategies
3. Discover communication techniques
4. Apply to relationships

---

## Conflict Causes

1. Miscommunication
2. Unmet needs
3. Different values
4. Past hurts
5. Stress and fatigue

---

## Resolution Strategies

1. Stay calm
2. Listen first
3. Identify the real issue
4. Find common ground
5. Work together on solution

---

## Communication Techniques

1. Use I statements
2. Avoid blame
3. Focus on feelings
4. Ask questions
5. Seek win-win solutions

---

## Action Checklist
- Practice calm communication
- Listen before responding
- Identify underlying issues
- Find common ground
- Work on solutions together
