# Chapter 34: Listening Habits

> Most people do not listen with the intent to understand, but with the intent to reply.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand listening importance
2. Learn listening levels
3. Discover listening barriers
4. Apply to conversations

---

## Listening Levels

1. Ignoring
2. Pretending
3. Selective
4. Attentive
5. Empathic

---

## Listening Barriers

- Distractions
- Judging
- Advising
- Rehearsing
- Daydreaming

---

## Better Listening

1. Focus fully
2. Observe nonverbal cues
3. Paraphrase
4. Ask questions
5. Summarize

---

## Action Checklist
- Practice empathic listening
- Minimize distractions
- Paraphrase to confirm understanding
- Ask open-ended questions
- Summarize conversations
