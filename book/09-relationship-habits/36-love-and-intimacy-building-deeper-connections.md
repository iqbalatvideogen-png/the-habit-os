# Chapter 36: Love and Intimacy

> Love is a decision, not an emotion.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand love and intimacy
2. Learn connection habits
3. Discover love languages
4. Apply to relationships

---

## Love Languages

1. Words of affirmation
2. Acts of service
3. Receiving gifts
4. Quality time
5. Physical touch

---

## Connection Habits

1. Daily affection
2. Weekly date nights
3. Monthly check-ins
4. Regular appreciation
5. Shared experiences

---

## Building Intimacy

1. Be vulnerable
2. Share feelings
3. Listen deeply
4. Spend quality time
5. Show appreciation

---

## Action Checklist
- Learn partner love language
- Practice daily connection habits
- Have weekly date nights
- Monthly relationship review
- Express appreciation regularly
