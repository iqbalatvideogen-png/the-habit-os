# Chapter 4: Systems vs Goals

> **You do not rise to your goals. You fall to your systems.**

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand goals vs systems
2. Learn why systems win
3. Design supporting systems
4. Apply systems thinking

---

## Why Systems Win

- Process over outcomes
- Continuous improvement
- You control actions
- Sustainable

---

## Environment Factor

Make good habits obvious and easy, bad habits invisible and hard.
