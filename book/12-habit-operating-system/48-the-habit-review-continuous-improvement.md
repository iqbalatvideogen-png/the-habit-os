# Chapter 48: The Habit Review

> Continuous improvement is better than delayed perfection.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand review importance
2. Learn review frameworks
3. Discover review questions
4. Apply to your system

---

## Why Review?

- Identify what is working
- Spot areas for improvement
- Celebrate progress
- Adjust strategies
- Stay motivated

---

## Review Frameworks

1. Daily review
2. Weekly review
3. Monthly review
4. Quarterly review
5. Annual review

---

## Review Questions

1. What went well?
2. What could be better?
3. What did I learn?
4. What will I do differently?
5. What am I grateful for?

---

## Action Checklist
- Schedule regular reviews
- Use review frameworks
- Answer review questions
- Adjust system based on insights
- Celebrate progress
