# Chapter 28: Financial Independence

> Financial freedom is the ultimate habit.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand financial independence
2. Learn FIRE principles
3. Discover habit connection
4. Apply to your life

---

## What is Financial Independence?

- Having enough to cover expenses
- Freedom to choose
- No reliance on employment
- Peace of mind

---

## FIRE Principles

- Financial Independence, Retire Early
- Save aggressively
- Invest wisely
- Live below your means
- Build passive income

---

## Habits for FI

1. Save consistently
2. Invest regularly
3. Avoid lifestyle inflation
4. Increase income
5. Reduce expenses

---

## The Math

- 4% rule
- 25x expenses
- Compound interest
- Time in market
