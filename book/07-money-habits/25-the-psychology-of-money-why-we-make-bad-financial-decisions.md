# Chapter 25: The Psychology of Money

> Money is a tool. Use it wisely.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand money psychology
2. Learn cognitive biases with money
3. Discover emotional money habits
4. Apply to better decisions

---

## Cognitive Biases

- Loss aversion
- Present bias
- Overconfidence
- Anchoring
- Mental accounting

---

## Emotional Money Habits

- Retail therapy
- Fear of missing out
- Keeping up with others
- Guilt spending
- Avoidance

---

## Better Money Decisions

1. Automate savings
2. Use the 24-hour rule
3. Track spending
4. Set financial goals
5. Educate yourself

---

## Action Checklist
- Identify money biases
- Automate savings
- Track all spending
- Set financial goals
- Review regularly
