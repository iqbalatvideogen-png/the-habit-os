# Chapter 27: Spending Habits

> Spend on what matters, save on what does not.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand spending psychology
2. Learn mindful spending
3. Discover value alignment
4. Apply to your spending

---

## Mindful Spending

1. Track all spending
2. Categorize by value
3. Identify waste
4. Align with priorities
5. Plan purchases

---

## Value-Based Spending

- Spend on experiences over things
- Spend on health and education
- Spend on relationships
- Avoid status spending
- Avoid impulse spending

---

## Spending Rules

1. 24-hour rule for non-essential purchases
2. One in, one out rule
3. Wait for sales
4. Buy quality
5. Avoid debt

---

## Action Checklist
- Track spending for 30 days
- Identify spending patterns
- Create spending plan
- Align with values
- Review monthly
