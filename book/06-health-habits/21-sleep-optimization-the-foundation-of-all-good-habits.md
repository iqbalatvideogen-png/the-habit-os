# Chapter 21: Sleep Optimization

> Sleep is the foundation of all good habits.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand sleep importance
2. Learn sleep optimization techniques
3. Discover sleep-habit connection
4. Apply to your life

---

## Why Sleep Matters

- Memory consolidation
- Neural repair
- Hormone regulation
- Energy restoration
- Emotional regulation

---

## Sleep Optimization Strategies

1. Consistent sleep schedule
2. Dark, cool, quiet environment
3. No screens before bed
4. Wind-down routine
5. Limit caffeine and alcohol

---

## Sleep and Habits

- Poor sleep reduces willpower
- Sleep deprivation makes habits harder
- Good sleep improves habit formation
- Naps can help but not replace night sleep

---

## Action Checklist
- Set consistent bedtime and wake time
- Create ideal sleep environment
- Establish wind-down routine
- Track sleep quality
- Optimize based on data
