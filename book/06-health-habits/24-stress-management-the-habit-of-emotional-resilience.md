# Chapter 24: Stress Management

> You cannot always control what happens, but you can control how you respond.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand stress impact
2. Learn stress management techniques
3. Discover stress-habit connection
4. Apply to your life

---

## Stress and Habits

- Chronic stress damages habit formation
- Stress reduces willpower
- Stress triggers bad habits
- Managing stress improves all habits

---

## Stress Management Techniques

1. Mindfulness meditation
2. Deep breathing
3. Exercise
4. Social connection
5. Sleep optimization

---

## Building Resilience

1. Practice gratitude
2. Develop growth mindset
3. Build social support
4. Maintain perspective
5. Take care of physical health

---

## Action Checklist
- Identify stress triggers
- Practice daily stress management
- Build resilience habits
- Track stress levels
- Adjust strategies as needed
