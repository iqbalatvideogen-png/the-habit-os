# Chapter 22: Nutrition Habits

> You are what you repeatedly eat.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand nutrition basics
2. Learn healthy eating habits
3. Discover nutrition-habit connection
4. Apply to your diet

---

## Key Nutrition Principles

- Eat whole foods
- Prioritize protein
- Include healthy fats
- Eat vegetables and fruits
- Stay hydrated

---

## Habit-Based Nutrition

1. Meal prep habit
2. Mindful eating habit
3. Hydration habit
4. Healthy snacking habit
5. Portion control habit

---

## Action Checklist
- Plan meals for week
- Prep ingredients in advance
- Eat mindfully without distractions
- Drink water throughout day
- Track nutrition habits
