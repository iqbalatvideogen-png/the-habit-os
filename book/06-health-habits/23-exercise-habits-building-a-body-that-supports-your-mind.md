# Chapter 23: Exercise Habits

> Take care of your body. It is the only place you have to live.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand exercise benefits
2. Learn how to build exercise habits
3. Discover exercise-brain connection
4. Apply to your routine

---

## Why Exercise?

- Physical health
- Mental health
- Cognitive function
- Energy levels
- Longevity

---

## Building Exercise Habits

1. Start small (2-minute rule)
2. Find enjoyable activities
3. Schedule it
4. Track progress
5. Gradually increase

---

## Exercise and Brain

- Boosts BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor)
- Improves neuroplasticity
- Reduces stress and anxiety
- Enhances memory and learning

---

## Action Checklist
- Choose exercise type you enjoy
- Start with 2 minutes daily
- Schedule exercise time
- Track workouts
- Gradually increase duration and intensity
