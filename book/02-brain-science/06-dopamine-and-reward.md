# Chapter 6: Dopamine, Reward, and the Modern Attention Economy

> **Dopamine is the currency of habit formation.**

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand dopamine role in habits
2. Learn about reward prediction error
3. Discover modern dopamine challenges
4. Apply to habit design

---

## Dopamine Basics

- Neurotransmitter for motivation and reward
- Released when we expect or receive rewards
- Drives us to repeat rewarding behaviors

---

## The Attention Economy

Modern apps exploit dopamine system:
- Variable rewards (likes, notifications)
- Infinite scroll
- Autoplay
- Gamification

---

## Reward Prediction Error

Brain releases dopamine when:
- Reward > Expectation (positive error)
- Reward < Expectation (negative error)

This drives learning and habit formation.

---

## Practical Applications

1. Design satisfying rewards
2. Create positive prediction errors
3. Avoid dopamine overload
4. Balance natural and digital rewards
