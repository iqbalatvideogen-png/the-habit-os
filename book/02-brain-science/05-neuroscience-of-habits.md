# Chapter 5: The Neuroscience of Habits

> **Your brain is the command center for all your habits.**

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand brain regions involved in habits
2. Learn how neural pathways form
3. Discover the habit circuit
4. Apply neuroscience to habit change

---

## Key Brain Regions

- Prefrontal Cortex: Decision making
- Basal Ganglia: Habit storage
- Amygdala: Emotional processing
- Nucleus Accumbens: Reward processing
- Hippocampus: Context and memory

---

## The Habit Circuit

Prefrontal Cortex -> Basal Ganglia -> Amygdala -> Nucleus Accumbens

The basal ganglia stores and executes habits automatically.

---

## Neuroplasticity

Your brain can rewire itself through practice and repetition.

---

## Practical Applications

1. Repetition strengthens neural pathways
2. Sleep consolidates learning
3. Stress damages habit circuits
4. Exercise boosts brain health
