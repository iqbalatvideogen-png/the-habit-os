# Chapter 7: The Role of Cortisol, Serotonin, and Other Key Neurotransmitters

> **Your brain chemistry determines your habit chemistry.**

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Understand cortisol in stress and habits
2. Learn serotonin role in mood and habits
3. Discover other key neurotransmitters
4. Apply to habit optimization

---

## Cortisol

- Stress hormone
- Chronic cortisol damages habit formation
- Reduces willpower and self-control
- Manage through exercise, sleep, mindfulness

---

## Serotonin

- Mood regulator
- Affects patience and long-term maintenance
- Boost through sunlight, exercise, healthy diet

---

## Other Neurotransmitters

- GABA: Calms brain, reduces anxiety
- Glutamate: Excites brain, essential for learning
- Oxytocin: Social bonding
- Endorphins: Natural painkillers
- Acetylcholine: Learning and memory
- Norepinephrine: Focus and alertness

---

## Practical Applications

1. Balance neurotransmitters naturally
2. Address imbalances through lifestyle
3. Use neurotransmitter-boosting activities
4. Create neurotransmitter-friendly environment
