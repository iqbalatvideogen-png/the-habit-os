---

## What is Neuroplasticity?

Brain ability to change structure and function throughout life.

---

## Types of Neuroplasticity

- Structural: Physical changes in brain structure
- Functional: Changes in how brain areas function
- Chemical: Changes in brain chemistry

---

## Habit Formation and Neuroplasticity

1. New synaptic connections form
2. Existing connections strengthen
3. Neural pathways become more efficient
4. Myelin sheath thickens
5. Basal ganglia takes over from prefrontal cortex

---

## BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)

- Protein that supports neuron growth
- Essential for neuroplasticity
- Boost through exercise, fasting, omega-3s
