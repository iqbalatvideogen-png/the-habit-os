The research referenced in this book represents the current state of knowledge in habit science and related fields as of the publication date. New research is constantly emerging, and we encourage you to stay informed about the latest developments.

The Habit OS is built on a foundation of rigorous research. Use these references to deepen your understanding and to explore the science behind the strategies that will transform your life.
