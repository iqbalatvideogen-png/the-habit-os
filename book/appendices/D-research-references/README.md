# Appendix D: Research References

> Complete bibliography with key studies and expert interviews

---

## About This Appendix

This comprehensive bibliography includes all the research, studies, books, articles, and expert sources referenced throughout The Habit OS. Organized by domain and chapter for easy reference.

---

## Domains Covered

### Psychology
Foundational research on behavior change, motivation, and habit formation

#### Key Contributors
- B.J. Fogg - Tiny Habits, behavior design
- James Clear - Atomic Habits, habit formation
- Charles Duhigg - The Power of Habit, habit loops
- Carol Dweck - Mindset, growth mindset
- Albert Bandura - Social Cognitive Theory, self-efficacy

#### Major Studies
- Lally et al. (2009) - How are habits formed: Modelling habit formation in the real world
- Wood & Neal (2016) - Healthy Through Habit: Interventions for Initiating & Maintaining Health Behavior Change
- Gollwitzer (1999) - Implementation intentions: Strong effects of simple plans

---

### Neuroscience
Research on brain mechanisms of habit formation and behavior change

#### Key Contributors
- Ann Graybiel - Basal ganglia, habit circuits
- Wolfram Schultz - Dopamine, reward prediction
- Michael Merzenich - Neuroplasticity
- Eric Kandel - Memory, synaptic plasticity

#### Major Studies
- Maguire et al. (2000) - Navigation-related structural change in the hippocampi of taxi drivers
- Draganski et al. (2004) - Neuroplasticity: changes in grey matter induced by training
- Kleim & Jones (2008) - Principles of experience-dependent neural plasticity

---

### Behavioral Economics
Insights into decision-making, biases, and habit-related behaviors

#### Key Contributors
- Daniel Kahneman - Thinking Fast and Slow, cognitive biases
- Richard Thaler - Nudge, behavioral economics
- Dan Ariely - Predictably Irrational
- Cass Sunstein - Nudge, choice architecture

#### Major Studies
- Thaler & Sunstein (2008) - Nudge: Improving Decisions About Health, Wealth, and Happiness
- Kahneman & Tversky (1979) - Prospect Theory: An Analysis of Decision under Risk

---

### Productivity
Research on focus, efficiency, and effective work habits

#### Key Contributors
- Cal Newport - Deep Work, focus
- David Allen - Getting Things Done, productivity systems
- Brian Tracy - Eat That Frog, time management
- Timothy Ferriss - The 4-Hour Workweek

---

### Health
Research on sleep, nutrition, exercise, and stress management

#### Key Contributors
- Matthew Walker - Why We Sleep
- Michael Pollan - Food rules
- Kelly McGonigal - The Willpower Instinct
