## How to Use These Resources

### For Wall Charts
- Print on large paper (11x17 or larger)
- Display in visible location
- Review daily
- Use as conversation starter

### For Desk Cards
- Print on cardstock
- Laminate for durability
- Keep on desk or in wallet
- Review regularly

### For Mobile Wallpapers
- Download to device
- Set as lock screen or home screen
- Change regularly to stay fresh
- Use as daily reminder

### For Tracking Sheets
- Print multiple copies
- Use for different time periods
- Keep in visible location
- Review and reflect regularly

## Design Tips

1. **Color Coding** - Use consistent colors for different categories
2. **Visual Hierarchy** - Make important information stand out
3. **White Space** - Avoid clutter, leave room for notes
4. **Consistency** - Use same style across all resources
5. **Personalization** - Add your own touch to make them yours

---

## Printing Tips

1. **Paper Quality** - Use thicker paper for durability
2. **Color vs Black & White** - Color for motivation, B&W for cost savings
3. **Lamination** - Protect frequently used resources
4. **Binding** - Create a habit binder with all your resources
5. **Digital Backup** - Keep digital copies for reprinting

---

## Customization

All resources are designed to be customizable:
- Edit text to match your habits
- Change colors to match your style
- Add your own graphics and images
- Combine elements from different resources
- Create your own variations
