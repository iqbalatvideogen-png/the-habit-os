# Appendix A: The Habit Diagnosis Test

> Assess your current habit landscape with 150 questions across 8 dimensions

---

## About This Test

This comprehensive assessment helps you understand your current habit strengths and weaknesses across multiple dimensions of your life.

---

## 8 Dimensions Assessed

1. **Habit Score** - Overall habit effectiveness
2. **Discipline Score** - Ability to stick to commitments
3. **Environment Score** - How your surroundings support habits
4. **Attention Score** - Focus and distraction management
5. **Motivation Score** - Drive and energy levels
6. **Sleep Score** - Sleep quality and quantity
7. **Nutrition Score** - Eating habits and health
8. **Digital Addiction Score** - Technology usage patterns

---

## How to Take the Test

1. Answer each question honestly
2. Rate yourself on a scale of 1-5
3. Calculate your scores for each dimension
4. Identify your strongest and weakest areas
5. Use results to guide your habit improvement journey

---

## Scoring

- 1 = Strongly Disagree / Never
- 2 = Disagree / Rarely
- 3 = Neutral / Sometimes
- 4 = Agree / Often
- 5 = Strongly Agree / Always

---

## Interpretation

- 40-50: Excellent - Strong habits in this area
- 30-39: Good - Solid foundation with room for improvement
- 20-29: Fair - Needs significant work
- Below 20: Poor - Urgent attention needed

---

## Next Steps

1. Review your scores
2. Identify 2-3 dimensions to focus on
3. Choose relevant chapters from The Habit OS
4. Create improvement plan
5. Track progress and retake test periodically

---

## Sample Questions

### Habit Score
- I consistently follow through on my commitments
- I have a morning routine that I stick to
- I make time for habits that are important to me

### Discipline Score
- I can resist temptations that conflict with my goals
- I do what I say I will do
- I follow through even when I do not feel like it

### Environment Score
- My environment supports my desired habits
- I have removed obstacles to good habits
- My surroundings make bad habits difficult
