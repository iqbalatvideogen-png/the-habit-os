# Appendix A: The Habit Diagnosis Test - 150 Questions to Assess Your Habit Strengths and Weaknesses

## Introduction

This comprehensive habit diagnosis test is designed to help you assess your current habit strengths, identify areas for improvement, and create a personalized habit development plan. Answer each question honestly, and use your results to guide your habit journey.

## How to Use This Test

1. Find a quiet place where you can focus
2. Answer each question thoughtfully and honestly
3. Score your responses using the provided scale
4. Tally your scores for each habit category
5. Identify your top 3 habit strengths
6. Identify your top 3 habit areas for improvement
7. Use your results to create a targeted habit development plan

## Scoring Guide

For each question, score your response on a scale of 1-5:
- 1 = Strongly Disagree / Never
- 2 = Disagree / Rarely
- 3 = Neutral / Sometimes
- 4 = Agree / Often
- 5 = Strongly Agree / Always
