# Chapter 40: The Habit of Vision

> The only limit to our realization of tomorrow is our doubts of today.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand vision importance
2. Learn vision creation
3. Discover vision habits
4. Apply to your future

---

## Why Vision Matters

- Provides direction
- Motivates action
- Aligns decisions
- Inspires others
- Creates legacy

---

## Vision Creation

1. Define values
2. Set long-term goals
3. Create vivid picture
4. Develop action plan
5. Review regularly

---

## Vision Habits

1. Daily vision review
2. Weekly planning
3. Monthly assessment
4. Quarterly adjustment
5. Annual retreat

---

## Action Checklist
- Define personal vision
- Create vision statement
- Review vision daily
- Align actions with vision
- Update vision regularly
