# Chapter 38: Decision-Making

> In any moment of decision, the best thing you can do is the right thing.

---

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand decision-making
2. Learn decision frameworks
3. Discover cognitive biases
4. Apply to better choices

---

## Decision Frameworks

1. Pros and cons list
2. Decision matrix
3. Cost-benefit analysis
4. SWOT analysis
5. 10-10-10 rule

---

## Cognitive Biases in Decisions

- Confirmation bias
- Anchoring
- Overconfidence
- Sunk cost fallacy
- Framing effect

---

## Better Decisions

1. Gather information
2. Consider alternatives
3. Weigh consequences
4. Seek input
5. Trust intuition

---

## Action Checklist
- Use decision frameworks
- Gather relevant information
- Consider multiple options
- Weigh pros and cons
- Make timely decisions
